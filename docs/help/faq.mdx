# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers about RightStockAI.

## General Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>What is RightStockAI?</strong></summary>

RightStockAI is an analysis-only stock research platform for Indian market users. It helps users research NSE and BSE stocks, compare analysis methods, review charts, track portfolios, monitor watchlists, and understand AI-supported research context.

RightStockAI is not a brokerage and does not provide personalized financial advice.

[View All Features](../features/stock-analysis)

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Which markets does RightStockAI focus on?</strong></summary>

RightStockAI is built for Indian market research, including NSE, BSE, NIFTY 50, Sensex, and supported listed stocks.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is RightStockAI free to use?</strong></summary>

Yes. RightStockAI has a Free plan for light usage, basic product learning, and selected previews.

Because plan allowances can change, this wiki does not copy exact Free-plan counts. See the [live Pricing page](https://www.rightstockai.com/pricing) for current allowances and [Plans and Access](../subscription/plans) for plain-language access terms.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does RightStockAI place trades?</strong></summary>

No. RightStockAI does not place buy or sell orders. It provides research tools and analysis context only.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is a Buy or Sell recommendation personal advice?</strong></summary>

No. A recommendation label is part of a research report. It is not personalized financial advice and should not be treated as an order instruction.

</details>

## Account and Login

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I create an account?</strong></summary>

1. Open Sign Up.
2. Enter the required account details.
3. Verify your email if prompted.
4. Sign in.
5. Open Dashboard, Analysis, Portfolio, Watchlist, or Subscription based on what you want to do.

For current signup, trial, and payment requirements, check the live Pricing page before choosing a plan.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I sign in without a password?</strong></summary>

Yes. Use "Email me a magic link" on the sign-in page. Enter your email address, open the newest email from RightStockAI, and click the link before it expires.

If the link is expired or already used, request a new one.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>My magic link expired. What should I do?</strong></summary>

Request a new magic link from the sign-in page, then use the newest email from RightStockAI. Magic links are time-sensitive and may work only once.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why am I redirected to sign in?</strong></summary>

Most product tools require login. You may be signed out, your browser session may have expired, or you may have opened a protected page in a different browser profile.

</details>

## Analysis Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>What is the difference between Traditional, Advanced AI, and Smart AI Analysis?</strong></summary>

Traditional Analysis is the finance-first report. It focuses on fundamentals, technical indicators, risk, analyst context, and scoring.

Advanced AI Analysis is the deeper AI-assisted stock report. It focuses on AI recommendation, confidence, target/risk context, and supporting reasons.

Smart AI Analysis blends Traditional, AI Predictive, and Sentiment inputs into one combined verdict.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What is Basic AI Analysis?</strong></summary>

Basic AI Analysis is a fast first-read tool. Use it when you want quick AI context before running deeper reports.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What is Chart Pattern Analysis?</strong></summary>

Chart Pattern Analysis detects chart setups, ranks them by confidence, explains the top-ranked pattern, and shows key levels such as support, resistance, target, or invalidation when available.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What is AI News Analysis?</strong></summary>

AI News Analysis summarizes stock-related news context, sentiment, catalysts, and risk points. Use it when recent news may explain price movement or report disagreement.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What is Stock Research Reports?</strong></summary>

Stock Research Reports is a report-browsing surface for recent shared reports and saved analysis access. Some plans may see previews instead of full details.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What are Smart AI Picks?</strong></summary>

Smart AI Picks is a premium shortlist where chart-pattern context, Smart analysis context, and final AI verdict overlap. It is different from the general reports feed.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What does confidence mean?</strong></summary>

Confidence means signal strength. It does not guarantee that a stock will move in the expected direction.

Read confidence together with:

- Recommendation.
- Supporting reasons.
- Risk notes.
- Chart context.
- News context.
- Report age.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why do two reports disagree?</strong></summary>

Different tools answer different questions. A company can have strong fundamentals but a weak chart setup. A chart can look bullish while news adds risk. A Smart AI report can be more cautious than base signals if valuation, technical stretch, or horizon mismatch weakens the thesis.

</details>

## Portfolio and Watchlist

<details>
<summary><strong>What is the difference between a portfolio and a watchlist?</strong></summary>

A portfolio is for holdings you own or model seriously as holdings. It is used for allocation, performance, exposure, and portfolio analysis.

A watchlist is for stocks you want to monitor or research. It does not represent ownership.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I add holdings to a portfolio?</strong></summary>

Use Portfolio Upload when you have a holdings file, or create/manage a portfolio from the Portfolio area. Make sure symbols, quantities, prices, and dates are formatted clearly.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why can I view an old portfolio report but not run a new one?</strong></summary>

Saved report access and fresh report generation are separate. Your plan may allow viewing a saved report while blocking a new run because of a daily limit or cooldown.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What should I do if portfolio upload fails?</strong></summary>

Check file type, required columns, stock symbols, dates, quantities, prices, blank rows, and merged cells. If needed, simplify the file and retry with fewer rows.

</details>

## Support

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I get help?</strong></summary>

Signed-in users can use Support inside the app. Public users can use Contact Us. Include your account email, tool name, stock symbol or portfolio name if relevant, and the exact error message.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Should I send screenshots?</strong></summary>

Screenshots help when the issue is visual, but avoid sharing screenshots publicly if they include email address, holdings, payment details, magic links, or private reports.

</details>
