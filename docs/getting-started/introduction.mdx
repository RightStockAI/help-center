# Welcome to RightStockAI

RightStockAI is a research platform for Indian market users who want structured stock analysis, AI-assisted interpretation, chart context, portfolio tools, and clear access guidance.

## Built For Indian Market Research

RightStockAI focuses on Indian equity market research and user workflows around supported NSE/BSE stocks, indices, portfolios, watchlists, and analysis reports.

| Area | What to use |
| --- | --- |
| Market day context | Markets |
| One-stock research | Stock detail, Traditional Analysis, Advanced AI Analysis, Smart AI Analysis |
| News and catalysts | AI News Analysis |
| Chart setup review | Chart Pattern Analysis and Charts |
| Idea discovery | Stock Research Reports and Smart AI Picks |
| Holdings review | Portfolio and Portfolio Analysis |
