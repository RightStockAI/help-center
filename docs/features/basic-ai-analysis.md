# Basic AI Analysis

Basic AI Analysis is the quickest AI-assisted stock check. It is useful when you want a first impression before using a daily analysis limit on deeper workflows.

It should be treated as a screening tool. It helps you decide whether a stock deserves more time, not whether you should buy or sell immediately.

## When To Use It

- Quick directional context.
- Early stock screening.
- Deciding whether a stock deserves deeper research.
- Comparing against the more complete reports later.
- Checking a watchlist name before opening a detailed report.
- Getting plain-language context when the market is moving quickly.

## What It Usually Tells You

- The stock being analyzed.
- The plain-language AI signal.
- Any visible risk or confidence context.
- Whether the result suggests a deeper Traditional, Advanced AI, or Smart AI report.

## What Basic AI Is Good For

Basic AI is best for early screening. It helps you decide whether to spend more time on a stock.

Good uses:

- First read before running deeper reports.
- Quick check on a watchlist stock.
- Comparing a few stocks before choosing one for deeper research.
- Getting plain-language context when a stock is moving.

Poor uses:

- Making a final decision from one short summary.
- Replacing Traditional or Smart AI Analysis.
- Ignoring news, chart context, or portfolio exposure.

## How To Read The Result

Read Basic AI in this order:

1. Confirm the stock name and symbol.
2. Read the main AI summary.
3. Check whether the tone is positive, cautious, neutral, or mixed.
4. Look for confidence or risk notes.
5. Decide whether deeper research is needed.

Do not stop at the headline. A positive first read can still include risk points, and a cautious first read can still include strengths.

## Example Reading Patterns

| Result pattern | How to think about it |
| --- | --- |
| Positive summary, no clear risk notes | Still compare Traditional Analysis before relying on it. |
| Positive summary, high risk | The idea may be interesting but fragile. |
| Mixed summary | Use Smart AI Analysis or AI News to understand the conflict. |
| Cautious summary | Read why it is cautious before ignoring the stock. |
| Unclear or thin context | Search the stock again later or use another tool. |

## Good Follow-Up Actions

| If Basic AI says... | Useful next step |
| --- | --- |
| Strong but risk is high | Open Traditional Analysis and check valuation, volatility, and drawdown. |
| Positive but news-sensitive | Open AI News Analysis and check recent catalysts. |
| Mixed or uncertain | Open Smart AI Analysis to compare Traditional, AI Predictive, and Sentiment signals. |
| Chart setup looks important | Open Chart Pattern Analysis or the symbol chart. |
| You already own it | Check the stock inside your portfolio context before acting. |

## Basic AI vs Other Analysis Tools

| Tool | Best use |
| --- | --- |
| Basic AI Analysis | Fast first read and screening. |
| Traditional Analysis | Finance-first report with fundamentals, valuation, technicals, and risk. |
| Advanced AI Analysis | Deeper AI-assisted stock research with more context. |
| Smart AI Analysis | Combined view across Traditional, AI Predictive, and Sentiment signals. |
| AI News Analysis | News sentiment, catalysts, and risk events. |

## What To Do After Basic AI

| Your situation | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Just learning the product | Add the stock to a watchlist and compare another view later. |
| Researching seriously | Open Traditional Analysis and Advanced AI Analysis. |
| Stock moved because of news | Open AI News before trusting the result. |
| You already own the stock | Check portfolio context before taking any action. |
| Result is old or unclear | Refresh context when available or use a newer report. |

## Common Mistakes

- Treating a quick AI summary as a complete report.
- Ignoring report age.
- Comparing a Basic AI result with a detailed report without checking the data time.
- Acting on a positive line without reading risk notes.
- Using Basic AI instead of portfolio-level analysis for holdings decisions.

## Best Practice

Use Basic AI as a starting point, not the final report. If a stock matters to you, follow up with Traditional Analysis, Advanced AI Analysis, Smart AI Analysis, AI News, and chart context.
