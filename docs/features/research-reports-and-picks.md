# Research Reports and Smart AI Picks

RightStockAI includes research discovery surfaces so users do not always have to start from a blank stock search.

## Stock Research Reports

Stock Research Reports help you browse:

- Recent shared reports.
- Saved analysis access.
- Report ideas generated from supported workflows.
- Analysis types such as Traditional, Advanced AI, and Smart AI.

Free users may see a limited preview in some shared report feeds. Your own saved analysis access can behave differently from the public/shared feed.

Use the live [RightStockAI Pricing](https://www.rightstockai.com/pricing) page for current access rules and plan allowances.

## Smart AI Picks

Smart AI Picks is a premium shortlist. It is not the same as the general reports feed.

Smart AI Picks highlights stocks where multiple signals overlap:

- Chart-pattern context.
- Smart analysis context.
- Final AI verdict.

## How To Use These Surfaces

1. Browse Reports or Smart AI Picks as discovery.
2. Open a stock only if the idea is relevant to you.
3. Check whether the displayed report is saved or current.
4. Run fresh analysis when you need updated context and your plan allows it.
5. Compare with chart, news, and portfolio exposure before acting.

## Common Confusion

| Confusion | Correct interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| "Reports are the same as Smart AI Picks" | No. Reports are broader; Smart AI Picks is a premium shortlist. |
| "A preview is a full report" | No. Preview access can hide details. |
| "A saved report is live market context" | No. Saved reports reflect the time they were generated. |
| "A curated idea is advice" | No. It is research discovery only. |

## How To Read Stock Research Reports

When browsing reports, check:

- Analysis type.
- Symbol.
- Report date.
- Recommendation or verdict.
- Confidence or score.
- Whether it is a preview or full report.
- Whether you generated it or it came from a shared feed.

## How To Read Smart AI Picks

Smart AI Picks should be read as a shortlist, not as an instruction list.

For each pick:

1. Open the detail.
2. Read why it was included.
3. Compare with current chart and news.
4. Check whether the signal is still fresh.
5. Consider portfolio concentration before treating it as relevant.

## Why Reports May Be Limited

Free plans may get previews in shared report feeds. This keeps the surface useful for discovery while reserving full details for plans with report access.

For current preview and upgrade behavior, use the live Pricing page and the signed-in Subscription page.

## Reports vs History vs Picks

| Surface | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Stock Research Reports | Discovery and browsing of recent/shared research surfaces. |
| Analysis History | Your generated or saved analysis history when your plan includes the surface. |
| Smart AI Picks | Curated premium shortlist where stronger signal overlap exists. |
| Stock detail report | The report for one selected symbol and analysis type. |

These surfaces may link to similar stocks, but they are not the same product feature.

## Freshness Checklist

Before relying on a report or pick, check:

- Report generation date.
- Analysis type.
- Whether market price moved since the report.
- Whether recent news changed the context.
- Whether the stock is already in your portfolio.
- Whether the page is showing preview or full access.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating a shared report as personalized advice.
- Assuming a pick is still fresh without opening details.
- Ignoring portfolio concentration because a stock appears attractive.
- Comparing a preview card with a full report.
- Mixing Smart AI Picks with the general reports feed.
