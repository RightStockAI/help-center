# Smart AI Analysis

Smart AI Analysis blends multiple signal families into one decision-oriented view. It is useful when you want one report that explains how different methods agree or disagree.

## Input Weights

The current Smart AI workflow presents these base inputs:

| Input | Weight | What it contributes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Traditional | 30% | Fundamentals, technicals, analyst context, and risk. |
| AI Predictive | 45% | AI-assisted forecast and predictive signal. |
| Sentiment | 25% | News and market sentiment context. |

The final Smart thesis can still be more cautious than the base inputs if valuation, technical stretch, horizon mismatch, or risk checks weaken the idea.

## What The Report Shows

- Final Smart AI verdict.
- AI summary.
- Supporting points.
- Caution points.
- Contribution breakdown.
- Agreement state: unanimous, majority, or split.
- Confidence and weighted contribution for each input.

## Agreement Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Unanimous | Traditional, predictive, and sentiment inputs point the same way. |
| Majority | Two inputs align while one adds caution. |
| Split | Inputs are pulling in different directions. |

## When To Use It

Use Smart AI when you want the fastest complete interpretation across multiple research styles. If the Smart AI result is surprising, open the individual Traditional, Advanced AI, and AI News tools to inspect the source of disagreement.

Use it especially when:

- Traditional Analysis looks strong but AI or sentiment is cautious.
- AI output is positive but valuation or risk is unclear.
- Recent news could be changing the stock view.
- You want to compare multiple methods without manually weighting them.
- You are reviewing a stock from Reports, Smart AI Picks, or a watchlist.

## Reading The Smart AI Report

Start with the final verdict, but do not stop there. The useful part of Smart AI is the explanation of how the inputs contributed.

Read in this order:

1. Final Smart AI verdict.
2. AI summary.
3. Supporting points.
4. Caution points.
5. Contribution breakdown.
6. Agreement state.
7. Individual input confidence.

## Example Interpretations

| Report situation | Layman-friendly reading |
| --- | --- |
| Unanimous and high confidence | Multiple inputs agree, but still check risk and report age. |
| Majority agreement | Two inputs agree, one adds caution. Read the caution before trusting the verdict. |
| Split agreement | The stock is unclear. Use the report to understand the conflict, not to force a decision. |
| Final thesis more cautious than inputs | Risk checks weakened the base signal. Read the caution section carefully. |

## Smart AI vs Smart AI Picks

These names sound similar, but they are different surfaces.

| Surface | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Smart AI Analysis | A report for one stock that blends Traditional, AI Predictive, and Sentiment inputs. |
| Smart AI Picks | A curated premium shortlist for discovery where multiple research signals align. |

If a stock appears in Smart AI Picks, still open the stock-level report and check whether the current Smart AI Analysis agrees.

## How To Handle Disagreement

Disagreement is not a failure. It is often the most useful part of the report.

| Conflict | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Traditional positive, sentiment negative | Read recent news and check whether the issue is temporary or structural. |
| AI predictive positive, valuation weak | Check whether the upside depends on momentum rather than fundamentals. |
| Chart bullish, Smart AI cautious | Look for risk, earnings, debt, or recent negative events. |
| Sentiment positive, Traditional weak | Avoid trusting headlines without company-quality support. |

## Common Mistakes

- Treating Smart AI as a guarantee because it combines methods.
- Ignoring the contribution breakdown.
- Reading only the final verdict.
- Forgetting sentiment can change quickly.
- Comparing Smart AI Picks with Smart AI Analysis as if they are the same feature.
- Ignoring a split result because the final label looks simple.
- Using Smart AI without checking report age.
