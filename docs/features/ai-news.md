# AI News Analysis

AI News Analysis helps users understand recent stock-related news without reading every article manually.

It is most useful when a stock has moved sharply, when a report changed tone, or when you want to know whether recent news supports or weakens the research view.

## What It Helps You Answer

- Is recent news positive, negative, or mixed?
- What catalysts are being discussed?
- What risks should I respect?
- Does the news context support or weaken the analysis view?
- Is the market reacting to company news, sector news, earnings, regulation, or broader sentiment?

## When To Open AI News

Open AI News when:

- A stock moved sharply.
- A report changed from positive to cautious or cautious to positive.
- Traditional Analysis and Smart AI Analysis disagree.
- A chart setup looks strong but you suspect news risk.
- The stock is in your portfolio and recent events may matter.

You do not need AI News for every stock every time. Use it when recent context could change interpretation.

## How To Use It

1. Search for the stock.
2. Read the summary.
3. Separate supporting catalysts from caution points.
4. Compare news context with Traditional and Smart AI reports.
5. Be careful when news is sparse, old, or fast-moving.

## What To Check First

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| News freshness | Old news may not explain current price movement. |
| Source coverage | Thin coverage can make the summary less complete. |
| Catalyst type | Earnings, orders, regulation, management changes, and sector news have different impact. |
| Sentiment direction | Positive, negative, and mixed news should be read differently. |
| Risk notes | Bad news can invalidate a strong chart or AI signal quickly. |

## News Categories

| Category | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Earnings or results | Can change confidence in business performance. |
| Orders or contracts | May affect growth expectations. |
| Regulation or policy | Can affect entire sectors. |
| Management commentary | Can change how investors read future prospects. |
| Sector news | May explain why many similar stocks moved together. |
| Market-wide news | Can move stocks even without company-specific events. |

## Important Limit

News analysis can summarize available information, but it cannot know every market-moving development instantly. Always check report time and recent market context.

## News Terms Explained

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Catalyst | A news event that may affect price or sentiment. |
| Sentiment | Whether news tone appears positive, negative, or mixed. |
| Risk event | News that can increase uncertainty or downside risk. |
| Market impact | How important the news may be for the stock. |
| Stale news | News that may no longer explain current movement. |
| Event risk | A pending or recent event that can change the stock view quickly. |
| Sector sentiment | News affecting a whole industry, not only one company. |

## How To Combine News With Reports

News is most useful when it explains why a stock moved or why other reports disagree.

Examples:

- A positive Traditional report with negative news needs caution.
- A bullish chart pattern after bad news may fail quickly.
- A weak AI report during temporary bad news may improve later, but only after evidence changes.

## Reading Sentiment Carefully

Sentiment is tone, not truth.

- Positive sentiment means recent coverage sounds supportive.
- Negative sentiment means recent coverage sounds cautious or risky.
- Mixed sentiment means the story is not clear.
- No recent news means other tools may matter more.

Do not assume sentiment predicts price. A stock can fall after good news if expectations were already high, and it can rise after bad news if the market expected worse.

## Reading Common News Situations

| Situation | How to think about it |
| --- | --- |
| Positive company news but weak market | The stock may still struggle if broader market pressure is high. |
| Good earnings but cautious outlook | Read management guidance before trusting the headline. |
| Bad news but price holds strong | Market may have already priced it in, but confirm with chart and volume. |
| Many small articles repeat the same point | Do not treat repeated coverage as multiple independent catalysts. |
| No recent news | Use Traditional, AI, and chart context more heavily. |

## Common Mistakes

- Treating sentiment as a price prediction.
- Ignoring whether the news is company-specific or sector-wide.
- Trusting old news during a fast-moving market.
- Reading only positive catalysts and skipping risk events.
- Using news alone without checking valuation, chart, and portfolio exposure.
