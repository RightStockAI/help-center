# NSE Stock Analysis Workflow

A good NSE stock analysis workflow should answer four questions: what is happening, why it may be happening, what could go wrong, and whether the setup fits your risk plan.

RightStockAI helps you organize those questions across stock pages, reports, charts, news, and portfolio tools. The goal is not to find a single magic score. The goal is to compare evidence before you rely on any view.

## Step 1: Start With The Stock Context

Open the stock or search for the company you want to study. Begin with broad context:

- Recent price behavior.
- Basic company and market information.
- Any available report history.
- Sector or index context when relevant.
- Whether the stock is already in your portfolio or watchlist.

If the stock is new to you, add it to a watchlist before you make it part of a portfolio plan.

## Step 2: Compare Research Views

Use more than one workflow when the decision matters:

- [Traditional Analysis](../../features/traditional-analysis) for finance-first and technical context.
- [Advanced AI Analysis](../../features/ai-analysis) for deeper AI-supported explanation.
- [Smart AI Analysis](../../features/smart-ai-analysis) for a combined view across signal types.
- [AI News Analysis](../../features/ai-news) for recent catalysts and sentiment.

Agreement across tools can be useful, but disagreement is often more important. If AI, technicals, and news point in different directions, review the caution points before forming a view.

## Step 3: Review Charts And Levels

Charts help you test whether the report matches current price behavior. Review:

- Trend direction and recent volatility.
- Support and resistance areas.
- Volume changes near breakouts or breakdowns.
- Pattern invalidation levels.
- Whether price has already moved far beyond the report setup.

For price-pattern workflows, read [Chart Pattern Analysis](../../features/chart-pattern-analysis) and [Chart Pattern Analysis for Indian Stocks](./chart-pattern-analysis-indian-stocks).

## Step 4: Check Risk Before Action

Before relying on the analysis, ask:

- Is the report fresh enough for the market move?
- Is confidence supported by clear reasons?
- What is the downside scenario?
- Does this stock increase concentration in one sector?
- Would a small position or watchlist monitoring be more appropriate?

For holdings-level review, use [Portfolio and Watchlist](../../features/portfolio-management) and [Portfolio Risk Analysis](./portfolio-risk-analysis).

## Key Takeaway

NSE stock analysis works best as a repeatable process. Use RightStockAI to collect evidence, compare workflows, and make risk visible before you act on any research output.
