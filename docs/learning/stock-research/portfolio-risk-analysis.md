# Portfolio Risk Analysis Basics

Portfolio risk analysis looks beyond one stock. It asks whether your holdings are too concentrated, too correlated, too volatile, or too dependent on one market theme.

RightStockAI portfolio tools help you organize holdings and review portfolio-level research context. The output is not personal financial advice. It is a structured way to notice risk before it becomes hard to manage.

## What To Check First

Start with basic portfolio structure:

- How many holdings do you have?
- Which sectors have the largest exposure?
- Are multiple holdings affected by the same news or macro risk?
- Is one stock too large compared with the rest?
- Are watchlist ideas adding diversification or more concentration?

A portfolio can look diversified by stock count but still be concentrated by sector, theme, or market factor.

## Use Reports As Risk Prompts

Portfolio analysis can surface useful prompts:

- Stocks with higher caution notes.
- Holdings where confidence is low or mixed.
- Positions affected by recent news.
- Stocks near important chart levels.
- Areas where a fresh report may be needed.

Use these prompts to decide what deserves deeper research. Do not treat the output as an automatic rebalance instruction.

## Compare Holdings And Watchlists

Watchlists are useful because they let you study ideas before they become real exposure. Before adding a watchlist stock to a portfolio, compare:

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Does it duplicate an existing sector? | Avoid hidden concentration |
| Is volatility higher than current holdings? | Understand position sizing risk |
| Is the report fresh? | Avoid stale analysis |
| Are signals mixed? | Slow down before relying on one view |
| What would invalidate the idea? | Define risk before upside |

For product usage, read [Portfolio and Watchlist](../../features/portfolio-management).

## A Simple Risk Review Routine

Use this routine weekly or before making a major change:

1. Review holdings by sector and position size.
2. Check fresh reports for the largest holdings.
3. Review watchlist names separately from owned holdings.
4. Compare AI, traditional, news, and chart context for any high-risk stock.
5. Write down the main risk in plain language.

If you cannot explain the risk clearly, do not rely only on the score or recommendation label.

## Key Takeaway

Portfolio risk analysis is about visibility. RightStockAI can help you organize holdings, compare signals, and notice concentration. Your final decision should still consider your goals, time horizon, risk tolerance, and professional advice where needed.
